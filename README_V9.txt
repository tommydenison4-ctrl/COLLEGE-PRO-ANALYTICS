GRIDLOCK COLLEGE GAMECAST V8

Fixes in this build:
- College Post a Play now uses the active NCAA matchup rather than the active NFL matchup.
- Dedicated Post a Play button added inside the college GameCast workspace.
- Posted-play ledger stores the correct NCAA matchup and NCAA game key.
- Current college simulator result can be added directly as a play leg.
- College simulator now includes margin histogram, total histogram, and simulated-score cloud.
- Heat-map/Power 4 lab is scoped to the selected game's two teams.
- Player filter is scoped to players from those two teams only.
- Closing the game resets the Power 4 lab to the complete population.


V9 TEAM COLORS
- College GameCast pulls primary/alternate team colors from ESPN schedule team data.
- Win-probability bars, simulator KPI accents, margin histograms, total distributions and score clouds use matchup colors.
- Similar/dark colors automatically fall back to a more legible secondary color.
- NFL simulator histograms now use active matchup team colors where the metric is team-specific.

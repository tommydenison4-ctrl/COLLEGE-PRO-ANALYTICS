GRIDLOCK College GameCast V7

College is a top-level GRIDLOCK league mode.

College slate:
- Live NCAA/FBS schedule feed
- Automatic GRIDLOCK projections
- Market-calibrated projection model when spread/total are available
- Conference and team search filters

Click any college game to open its GameCast workspace:
- GAMECAST: matchup header, projected score, win probability, game status
- PROJECTION: model score, margin, total, team-strength inputs, calibration context
- PREDICTIONS: straight-up result plus model-vs-market spread and total edges
- SIMULATOR: browser Monte Carlo runs, win rate, cover rate, over/under rate
- ANALYTICS: team ratings and link into the Power 4 spatial matchup lab
- CHAT: game-specific room with local fallback until Supabase credentials are connected

Power 4 spatial data and existing NFL modules remain included.

GRIDLOCK V14
- Post a Play opens a prediction/market picker for active college games.
- GAME: away ML/winner, home ML/winner, projected score.
- SPREAD: GRIDLOCK spread lean when a market spread is available.
- OVER / UNDER: both sides of the current total are selectable.
- PLAYER PROPS: reserved category for upcoming QB/RB/WR/TE prop projections.
- Selecting any option opens the existing quick-share card prefilled; no typing required.
- Users can go back and choose another play.
